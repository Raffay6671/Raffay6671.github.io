Outreach

AI-powered outreach across email and LinkedIn that knows when to stop.

Recruiters build one sequence that mixes LinkedIn invites, messages and InMail with Gmail and Outlook emails. AI drafts every message for the person receiving it and reads every reply, and the moment someone answers on any channel, every pending follow-up stops.

ReactTypeScriptNode.jsExpressPostgreSQLRedisBullMQNext.jsSocket.IOChrome MV3
4 channels

Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn and Recruiter InMail in one sequence

7 touch types

From invites and InMail to post likes and profile views

6 reply intents

Told apart by AI, from interested to wrong contact

1 API

Every send and every reply goes through the Message Hub

Multi-channel sequences

Mix LinkedIn invites, messages, post likes, profile views, Recruiter InMail, Gmail and Outlook in one timed sequence, with attachments where the channel allows them.

AI

AI-personalized copy

Every message is drafted by AI for the person receiving it, and stays editable in a rich-text editor before anything is sent.

AI

Reply intelligence

AI reads each reply and sorts it: interested, not interested, out of office, deferred, wrong contact or ambiguous.

Stops on any reply

A reply on LinkedIn stops the Gmail and Outlook follow-ups too, so nobody gets chased after they've already answered.

Idempotent Message Hub

One service owns every send and reply. An idempotency key travels through every hop, so a retry can never deliver the same message twice.

Native integrations

Gmail API and Microsoft Graph over OAuth 2.0 with native threading, plus a Manifest V3 Chrome extension that runs LinkedIn actions pushed over Socket.IO.

How a message travels

The same idempotency key rides along every hop, so any hop can retry safely.

Transcriber

Every call captured, transcribed and turned into notes you can act on.

Transcriber joins Google Meet, Zoom and Teams calls straight from your calendar or a link, records phone calls through a Twilio number, and takes uploaded recordings. Every conversation becomes a speaker-labelled transcript, an AI summary and notes in your own template, which you can reshape just by asking the AI.

3 platforms

1 doc per person

4 GB uploads

Audio or video recordings, uploaded straight to cloud storage

Joins from your calendar

Connect Google or Outlook and a bot named after you joins each Meet, Zoom or Teams call on its own, or send it into any live meeting by link.

Phone calls, too

Dial the Transcriber number, key in who to call, and the call is bridged, recorded and transcribed. Your number is verified with Twilio caller ID.

AI

Notes in your format

AI writes notes for each attendee in their chosen template. Build templates from scratch, from a note you liked, or by uploading a document.

AI

Edit with QLU

Ask the AI to summarize, pull out action items or rewrite for a client. It edits the notes in place, and every version can be applied or undone.

Jump to the moment

Every note section links to the exact clip it came from, played next to the matching lines of the transcript.

Search, share and export

Search every transcript, share notes across your company or with specific people, and export notes or transcripts to PDF.
